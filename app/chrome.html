TaskM Chrome button

Some homework lives on pages that need your school sign-in, like a class OneNote, Teams, Aeries grades or your school email. Open the page and click the TaskM button once. TaskM finds the homework and grades and keeps them updated from then on. You stay signed in to your school as usual, and TaskM never sees your school password.

Install it (computer, Chrome)

  1. Download TaskM Chrome
    Open the downloaded file. You'll get a folder called TaskM Chrome.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions by copying it into the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the TaskM Chrome folder.
  5. Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to TaskM, so the button stays visible.

Use it

  1. Open the page with your homework, like your teacher's OneNote page, and wait for it to load.
  2. Click the TaskM button, or press Option+Shift+T on a Mac (Alt+Shift+T on Windows).
  3. TaskM opens and adds the homework and grades it finds.
  4. That's it. TaskM keeps the page updated by itself: every time you open it, and once a day in the background while Chrome is open.

Good pages to use it on

  1. Teams: click the button anywhere in Teams and TaskM finds every class’s assignments by itself. Also your class OneNote, Google Classroom to-do, Pearson or AP Classroom.
  2. Your grades page, like Aeries. Your grades in TaskM then stay current.
  3. Your email. In TaskM, open the Email tab and click Link Gmail, Link school Outlook or Link Outlook.com. TaskM reads your school and personal email in the background, sums up each one in the Email tab, and checks again every day. For another email account, open its inbox in Chrome and click the button there.

The button reads a page only when you click it, or when it refreshes a page you clicked it on before. If a school sign-in runs out, TaskM tells you which page to sign in to again. Keep the TaskM Chrome folder where it is, since Chrome loads the button from it. Privacy
