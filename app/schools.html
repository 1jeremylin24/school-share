TaskM for schools

TaskM is a homework planner made by a PVPHS student for Palos Verdes Peninsula High School. It gathers each student’s homework from OneNote, Teams, Aeries, school email and the PVPHS website into one list, so fewer assignments slip through.

For students

  • Tonight’s top 3 and a 7-day view
  • Heavy-day warnings before test pile-ups
  • Grades, curves and “what do I need on the final?”
  • School news filtered to their grade

For parents

  • A Sunday email with the week ahead
  • Grades and which way they moved
  • Only after the student shares an invite code

For staff

  • Post an announcement to one grade or everyone; it shows in every student’s School tab
  • See which days have the most tests, by grade, with no names

For the school

  • No cost to PVPHS for a pilot
  • No school passwords stored, no ads, no selling data
  • Works with what PVPHS already uses

How TaskM protects student data

  • Students control it. Each student makes their own account and chooses what to connect. TaskM is for students 13 and older.
  • No school passwords. TaskM never asks for, types or keeps a student’s school password. Pages are read through the browser or iPhone app the student is already signed in to.
  • Only for school use. Student data is used only to run TaskM for that student. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to build profiles for anything outside TaskM. This is what California’s Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA) requires.
  • Who can see what. A student’s homework and grades are visible to the student, to a parent they link with a one-time invite code, and to no one else. Staff see only counts (no names or classes), and only for grades with at least 5 TaskM students.
  • Where it lives. Data is stored in Google Firebase in the United States. Homework text a student shares is read by Anthropic’s Claude AI, which does not train on it.
  • Deleting. A student or parent can delete an account and everything TaskM keeps about it by emailing taskm.help@gmail.com.
  • Records requests. Parents can ask for a copy of their student’s TaskM data at the same address, as FERPA allows for education records.

Full details are on the privacy page. We’re glad to go through the district’s student data privacy review.

One thing that would help every student

PVPUSD requires IT approval before students can sign in to outside apps with their school Microsoft account. Approving TaskM’s Microsoft app would let students connect Teams, OneNote and Outlook in one step, without the Chrome button.
